Why Dr.0579 English Class is Effective

At Dr.0579 English Academy, students do not simply memorize English for tests. They learn how to build, complete, and continue English sentences naturally through the original Peacemaker English Teaching Theory, developed by Joonyeong Oh, Director of Dr.0579.

The Peacemaker system is a structured speaking method based on Cowboy Shooting English — a visual six-bullet sentence-building system that trains students to speak English quickly, accurately, and confidently.

Just as a skilled cowboy loads and fires each bullet in the correct order, students learn to construct English sentences in the correct phrase order, allowing them to speak without hesitation.

Why Peacemaker Is Different

Most Korean students first think in Korean and then translate word by word into English. This often causes hesitation, unnatural sentence order, and slow speaking.

The Peacemaker method changes this process completely. Students learn to:

  • Load Bullet 1 first: Subject + Verb Phrase, the grammatical core of every sentence.
  • Add meaningful phrases: object, place, time, reason, method, and other necessary information.
  • Finish the sentence naturally: by answering the necessary 5W1H questions.
  • Reload and continue speaking: using transition words such as and, but, so, because, however, and therefore.

Because students focus on the next meaningful phrase instead of translating every Korean word, they develop the ability to speak English in real time.

The Dr.0579 Speaking Sequence

"Draw. Load. Fire. Reload. Speak English at bullet speed."

This slogan does not mean speaking carelessly or too fast. It means students become able to produce English smoothly and continuously without long pauses.

Stage What Students Learn
Draw Understand the situation and choose the main idea.
Load Build Bullet 1 and add the next meaningful phrase.
Fire Speak a complete, natural sentence.
Reload Connect the next sentence with a transition word.
Continue Create longer conversations, explanations, and stories.

The Six-Bullet Sentence System

The Peacemaker Revolver gives students a clear visual map for sentence building.

Bullet Function
1 Subject + Verb Phrase — the grammatical core
2 Object, complement, place, time, or needed phrase
3 The next meaningful phrase
4 The next meaningful phrase
5 The next meaningful phrase
6 The next meaningful phrase

Students always begin with Bullet 1, ensuring that every sentence has a correct grammatical foundation. Then they add only the information necessary to complete the idea naturally.

A Finished Sentence, Not a Memorized Sentence

In Peacemaker, a sentence is finished when the student has answered the necessary 5W1H questions naturally.

Question Purpose
Who? Subject
What? Action or object
Where? Place
When? Time
Why? Reason or purpose
How? Manner or method

Example

I study English at school every day to improve my speaking skills.

This sentence naturally answers Who, What, Where, When, and Why, making it complete, meaningful, and natural.
